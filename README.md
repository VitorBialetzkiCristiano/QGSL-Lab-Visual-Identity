# QGSL Lab – Visual Identity

QGSL Lab – Visual Identity

Repository dedicated to organizing, documenting, and sharing visual identity materials developed for the Quantitative Genetics and Statistical Learning Laboratory (QGSL Lab), affiliated with the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Computational Intelligence (IDATA) at the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV), Brazil.

The repository brings together graphic resources, visual elements, and communication materials used in the laboratory's scientific outreach and institutional communication, promoting consistency, organization, and accessibility for laboratory members and the academic community.

Developed by Vitor Bialetzki Cristiano (QGSL Lab member) under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Kaio Olímpio, this repository is publicly available as a contribution to the laboratory's visual identity, institutional communication, and dissemination of its scientific activities.
